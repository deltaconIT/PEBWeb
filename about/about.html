01Who we are

The Philippine arm of SEICO Group

PEB Philippines is the Philippine local marketing arm and strategic implementation partner of SEICO Group, one of Southeast Asia's leading manufacturers and fabricators of Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB). Established to expand SEICO Group's presence in the Philippine market, PEB Philippines brings world-class steel building solutions closer to Filipino developers, contractors, industrial companies and business owners.

With the growing demand for industrial facilities, warehouses, factories, commercial buildings and other large-scale steel structures, we aim to provide the Philippine construction industry with cost-efficient, engineered and high-quality PEB solutions, backed by international manufacturing expertise and reliable local execution.

02Our partnership

SEICO Group manufactures. DELTA Group builds.

Recognizing the increasing demand for PEB projects in Vietnam and the growing opportunities in the region, SEICO Group began expanding its international market presence in 2015. It entered the Philippines in the last quarter of 2022, strengthening its position as a major PEB manufacturer and fabricator in Southeast Asia.

To establish a strong and reliable presence in the Philippines, SEICO Group partnered with DELTA Group, operating under DELTA DNC Inc., a PCAB Category B licensed contractor, for local construction expertise and project implementation. PEB Philippines and DELTA Group share the same leadership and work hand in hand on every project.

This strategic partnership combines SEICO Group's manufacturing capabilities with DELTA Group's local engineering, construction and project management experience.

03What we do

One integrated approach to PEB projects

Through our partnership, PEB Philippines covers every stage of a Pre-Engineered Building project.

  1. 01

    PEB manufacturing and supply

    Internationally manufactured structural steel components and building systems from SEICO Group.

  2. 02

    Design collaboration and engineering

    Coordination and collaboration to make sure project designs comply with applicable Philippine engineering requirements, including the National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP 2015).

  3. 03

    Erection and installation

    Professional local erection and installation services through DELTA Group.

  4. 04

    General construction

    Local construction and project coordination through our PCAB-licensed contracting partner.

  5. 05

    Importation and logistics

    Management of importation, shipping coordination, customs processing and delivery arrangements.

  6. 06

    Direct-to-site delivery

    Direct unloading at the project site, or delivery to your preferred storage facility when site access or staging conditions are challenging.

Successful PEB projects take more than quality steel. They need proper engineering coordination, efficient logistics, experienced installation teams and dependable local project execution.

06Quality, health, safety & environment

QHSE

The same standards apply in SEICO Group's factories and on our project sites in the Philippines.

Quality

With the motto "selling value, not just products", we focus on the quality of both our buildings and our service. Steel members are fabricated in SEICO Group's factories under an ISO 9001:2015 quality management system and SEICO's inspection and testing plan, covering material checks, dimensions, welding tests and paint thickness. Designs are reviewed by our Filipino structural engineer for compliance with NSCP 2015.

  • Affordability
  • Quality team
  • Safe construction
  • Excellent planning

Health

We work to give our people a healthy and safe working environment, in the factories and on site. Teams are trained regularly on the health and safety requirements of their work, and equipment is run under strict technical and administrative controls, with regular maintenance and safe work procedures.

  • Health and safety policies follow current government regulations and are updated as those change, including hazardous materials management and injury reduction.
  • Employee benefits, working hours, holidays and leave follow the applicable labour regulations.
  • Workplaces are inspected periodically, and teams have adequate protective equipment and facilities.

Safety

Keeping our people safe is everyone's responsibility, and the highest responsibility rests with the managers assigned to occupational safety. Pre-engineered buildings also make sites safer: members arrive cut, drilled and painted, and are bolted together, so there is far less welding and cutting on site.

  • Supervisors check the work area every day, stay on site for the whole working time, know the work they supervise and may stop work if they find life-threatening conditions or faulty equipment.
  • Workers follow the safety standards and rules of the site; temporary workers follow the site standards and their supervisor's instructions.
  • Everyone on site is fully equipped with protective equipment.

Environment

A clean, tidy workplace prevents accidents, keeps people healthy and helps them focus. Factory-controlled fabrication also means less waste and fewer consumables on site.

  • Everyone keeps the workplace clean and tidy, with equipment stored in safe, designated areas.
  • Offcuts and small metal pieces from grinding are kept in designated areas.
  • Work areas are cleaned before work starts and inspected regularly; leftover materials are removed or stacked daily.
  • Fuel, chemical and hazardous spills are cleaned up straight away, and waste goes into the proper containers.
