04Why pre-engineered

Less steel, smaller foundations, faster on site

How a pre-engineered building compares with a conventional steel building.

  • 15–40%

    Lighter main frame

    High-strength steel and tapered sections cut the quantity of columns and rafters.

  • 15–30%

    Lighter secondary frame

    High-strength cold-formed purlins and girts reduce the secondary steel.

  • Smaller foundations

    A lighter structure with longer bay spacing puts less force on the foundations, so fewer are needed.

  • Bolted, not welded

    Members arrive cut to size and bolt together on site with no welding, which keeps erection fast and clean.
