SP Deck floor sheet

An integrated composite floor with a profiled base sheet and a factory-welded steel truss, for faster floors and longer spans.

SP Deck is an integrated composite floor system manufactured by SEICO Group, consisting of a profiled base sheet and a triangular steel truss fabricated and welded in the factory. During construction, the base sheet acts as permanent formwork and works with the steel truss to form the floor before concrete is poured. Once the concrete reaches its design strength, the components work with the beams as designed to form a complete floor structure. By integrating the steel truss into the floor sheet, SP Deck reduces rebar installation on site and shortens construction time. This page explains SP Deck's structure, advantages and installation process.

Product consultation: 0917-832-0004

1. What is SP Deck?

SP Deck is manufactured by SEICO Group on an imported line to JG/T 368:2012 (equivalent to JIS G 3532:2011). It uses coated steel sheet to AS 1397 and JIS G 3302, combined with steel bars to TCVN 1651-2:2018.

SP Deck floors are calculated and designed according to the following technical standards:

  • Load calculation standards: TCVN 2737:1995 and Eurocode 1.
  • Floor structure design standards: TCVN 5574:2012, Eurocode 2, Eurocode 4 and AISC 360-10.

For projects in the Philippines, the floor is designed and checked to NSCP 2015 together with the rest of the building.

2. What are the main components of SP Deck?

SP Deck consists of two main parts: the base sheet and the triangular steel truss. Each part has its own function, improving load capacity and streamlining deck floor construction.

1. Base sheet:

The SP Deck base sheet serves these functions:

  • Replaces the bottom formwork when pouring concrete
  • Retains water and concrete mortar
  • Creates a connection with the steel frame

The sheet surface is corrugated and embossed to improve concrete bond and provide welding points for the steel truss.

2. Triangular steel truss system:

The steel truss is the main load-bearing part of the SP Deck sheet. Its triangular design serves to:

  • Distributes loads more evenly
  • Increases floor stiffness
  • Reduces deflection and vibration
  • Overcomes the vibration weakness of steel buildings

Combining the base sheet and the triangular steel truss, SP Deck can span long distances, reducing the number of secondary beams and optimizing load capacity and construction costs for steel structure.

3. Outstanding advantages of the SP Deck composite floor

The SP Deck composite floor offers efficient construction with fast installation, long spans, consistent quality and lower finishing costs for steel frame buildings, especially pre-engineered steel building.

  • Exceptionally fast construction: Because the reinforcement is fabricated and welded in the factory, SP Deck minimizes bottom-layer rebar work on site, making floor construction at least 30% faster than standard deck floors.
  • Long secondary beam spans: SP Deck allows secondary beam spans of more than 4 m without props. This significantly reduces secondary beam steel and saves on materials and labor.
  • Attractive appearance and lower ceiling finishing costs: With an almost flat base sheet profile, the finished ceiling surface looks far better than deck floor traditional ones. Investors can skip a false ceiling altogether to save space and cost.
  • Consistent, easy-to-control quality: Steel fabrication is carried out by precise automated machinery, minimizing manual errors and making quality control and acceptance easy.

4. SP Deck floor sheet types

SEICO Group's SP Deck composite floor comes in 3 product lines, W400, W563 and W603, which differ in steel truss design, effective width, floor height and deck base sheet thickness. Each has its own specifications for different design and construction needs.

4.1. SP DECK - W400

SP Deck W400 consists of:

  • Number of steel trusses: 2 trusses.
  • Effective width: 400 mm.
  • Floor sheet height: 100 ~ 270 mm.
  • Deck base sheet thickness: 0.5 mm.
  • Galvanizing standard: Z80 – Z275.
  • Stiffness (base steel strength): G300.

4.2. SP DECK - W563

SP Deck W563 is designed with:

  • Number of steel trusses: 3 trusses.
  • Effective width: 563 mm.
  • Floor sheet height: 90 ~ 270 mm.
  • Deck base sheet thickness: 0.5 ~ 0.75 mm.
  • Galvanizing standard: Z80 – Z275.
  • Stiffness (base steel strength): G300.

4.3. SP DECK - W603

SP Deck W603 consists of:

  • Number of steel trusses: 3 trusses.
  • Effective width: 596 mm.
  • Floor sheet height: 100 ~ 270 mm.
  • Deck base sheet thickness: 0.5 ~ 0.75 mm.
  • Galvanizing standard: Z80 – Z275.
  • Stiffness (base steel strength): G300.

5. SP Deck installation process

SP Deck installation follows a sequence from transport and laying the sheets to fixing, reinforcement and pouring the concrete. Each step must follow the approved shop drawings to ensure accuracy and floor quality.

  • Step 1: Lift the SP Deck bundles onto the structural floor.
  • Step 2: Lay the SP Deck sheets according to the approved shop drawings.
  • Step 3: Weld the SP Deck sheet ends down to the secondary beams
  • Step 4: Weld shear studs and lay the anti-crack steel mesh on top.
  • Step 5: Install concrete edge stops for the floor.
  • Steps 6 & 7: Pour concrete and finish the surface

6. Detailed comparison: SP Deck vs. traditional Form Deck floors

SP Deck and traditional Form Deck floors differ in structure, construction method, span and finishing requirements. The table below clarifies the characteristics of each so you can choose the right option for your project:

Form deck SP Deck
Construction Slow construction due to time spent laying and fixing floor sheets At least 30% faster construction than standard deck
Quality Hard to control quality Fabrication of folded plate beams without errors and with easy quality control
Aesthetics Large floor deflection, poor appearance The flat base sheet looks clean and attractive, so a separate ceiling can be skipped
Span Short span, max 2.5 m only. Spans of more than 4 m reduce secondary beam tonnage and save costs.
