01Local partner

DELTA Group, our partner on the ground

A growing construction and building solutions group in the Philippines, committed to delivering innovative, cost-efficient and high-quality construction solutions.

PEB Philippines and DELTA Group share the same leadership and work hand in hand on every project. DELTA Group's services range from design, general construction and project management to the importation of pre-engineered steel buildings, innovative masonry materials and modular housing solutions.

Through its strategic partnership with SEICO Group, DELTA Group gives clients a single, reliable local partner who oversees the entire process, from the procurement and importation of steel components to their delivery and professional installation on site, with applicable product and structural warranties, technical coordination and local project support.

The group

  • DELTA DNC Inc.The group's pioneer company. Established in February 2018 and licensed by PCAB under Category B, it grew from residential and commercial fit-outs into industrial, commercial and multipurpose building construction.
  • DELTACON IncorporatedRegistered in May 2026 as a general construction company and importer of innovative building materials and structural systems.
  • PEB, AAC, modularKey business segments: pre-engineered buildings, autoclaved aerated concrete blocks and modular housing solutions.
