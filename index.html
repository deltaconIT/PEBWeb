02How we deliver

One team, from the first drawing to the last bolt

  1. 01 Design Filipino engineers set the design parameters to NSCP 2015, SEICO Group's engineers model and analyze the building, and a Filipino structural engineer reviews the design.
  2. 02 Manufacturing Every member is made in SEICO Group's own factories in Vietnam, with materials tested and each stage checked by QA/QC engineers.
  3. 03 Installation DELTA Group's PCAB-licensed crews and supervising engineers erect the building on site exactly to the design, from columns to cladding.

03Who we are

About PEB Philippines

PEB Philippines is the Philippine local marketing arm and strategic implementation partner of SEICO Group, one of Southeast Asia's leading manufacturers and fabricators of pre-engineered buildings. We bring world-class steel building solutions closer to Filipino developers, contractors, industrial companies and business owners.

Together with DELTA Group, our partner under the same leadership, we handle every stage locally: design to NSCP 2015, supply from SEICO Group's factories, importation, delivery and installation.
